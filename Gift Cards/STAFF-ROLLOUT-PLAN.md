# Virtual Gift Cards — Staff Plan

**Launch:** week of Sept 29, 2026
**Buy page:** https://velocitysimlounge.com/gift-cards/
**System:** RoverD

---

## The basics

- Guests buy gift cards online for Houston or Dallas. No store visit, no mailing.
- The recipient gets an email with a gift code. Nothing physical.
- Cards pay for racing only. Not food, drinks, or merch.
- Cards expire 1 year from purchase. The date is on the email.

## How it works

**Buying:** Guest picks Houston or Dallas and an amount ($25–$500 or custom) on the gift card page. Checkout is RoverD's gift card page in a popup. Buyer enters their name and email, plus the recipient's name, email, and a note. If they leave the recipient email blank, the code comes to them instead. Code emails right after payment.

**Redeeming:** The recipient books a race online as normal and enters the code in the **"Giftcard / Discount code"** box at payment. The card covers that booking; anything over the card value is paid by credit card.

**Location:** Houston and Dallas are separate RoverD systems. A Houston code only works on Houston bookings, a Dallas code only on Dallas bookings.

**At check-in:** Nothing new. The booking is already paid. RoverD shows the gift card on the booking details.

## RoverD Admin → Gift Card tab

- Search by code, name, or email.
- You see the status, code, amount, expiration, and last used.
  - **Active** = good to use · **Redeemed** = already used · **Expired** = past 1 year · **Initialized / Awaiting** = never paid, not valid
- Managers can **resend the email**, **issue a card at the counter**, or **bulk-create** cards for corporate orders.

## What to say

| Guest says | You do |
|---|---|
| "Can I buy a gift card?" | Point them to velocitysimlounge.com/gift-cards/. Takes two minutes on their phone. Want a printout? Leave the recipient email blank and print the email. |
| "Can I pay for it here?" | Get a manager. They issue it from RoverD and take payment. |
| "Can I use it for food/drinks?" | No, racing only. Book a race with it, pay for food separately. |
| "I have a code but haven't booked." | Check availability, then have them book online (their phone or a lounge tablet) and enter the code at payment. |
| "I never got the email." | Check spam. Look them up in RoverD and hit Resend. |
| "My code isn't working." | Check RoverD: Expired? Already used? Wrong location? Otherwise get a manager. |
| "I have a Dallas card but I'm in Houston." | Don't turn them away. Manager verifies it, issues a matching card here, and deletes the original. |
| "I have an old physical card." | Honor it like always. |

## Still to decide (managers)

- Cross-location: re-issue at the counter (as written above) or hold guests to the location they bought for.
- Physical cards: keep selling or phase out.
- Expired cards: grace period or hard no.
- Does a leftover balance carry to a future booking? Test a card and find out.
