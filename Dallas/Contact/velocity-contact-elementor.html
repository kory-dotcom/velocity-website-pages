About Velocity Dallas

Velocity Dallas is the largest simulation racing entertainment destination in Texas. With over 10,000 square feet of space and room for 400 guests, our 32 professional-grade simulators deliver an experience unlike anything else in DFW. Grab a drink from our full kitchen and bar, fuel up with chef-crafted shareables, and compete with friends on iconic circuits from around the world. Located at The Hill, Velocity Dallas is where racing meets hospitality.
