How it works

From sign-up to the podium

The whole season, start to finish: what happens when you register, what a race night looks like, how the standings work, and what we ask of every driver.

Jump to entry & pricing
  1. Now through Oct 18

    Register

    $225 + tax secures your seat for all six Mondays. There are 42 seats in total, 21 in each class. Class placement happens at Week 1 check-in, so checkout is one step: pay, and you're on the grid.

  2. Mon Oct 19 · 6:00 PM

    Week 1 check-in

    Arrive by 6 PM. The Pit Crew places you in Rookies or Pro based on your experience, you pick the one GT3 car you'll drive all season, and you draw your rig for the night.

  3. Every Monday · 6–9 PM

    Race night

    Check in at 6, drivers' briefing at 6:30, Rookies race at 7, Pro race at 8. Each week is a new circuit. Rigs are drawn fresh every night and every car runs the same fixed setup, so the result comes down to driving.

  4. After every round

    Points & standings

    Every finishing position scores championship points. Results, standings, and fastest laps for both classes go up on the public leaderboard after each race night. After Race 1 we may move drivers between classes so both grids stay fair.

  5. Mon Nov 23 · Sonoma

    Finale & awards

    The last race decides it. A champion is crowned in Rookies and in Pro, and trophies and prizes are handed out at Velocity Dallas the same night.

The season

6 weeks. 6 circuits. One champion.

Every Monday brings a new track — from the high-speed Glen to the Corkscrew at Laguna Seca. Pick a week to explore the layout and what it takes to be quick there.

Week 1 · Long course (“The Boot”)
Week 1 · Mon Oct 19 · 7:00 PM

Watkins Glen International

Watkins Glen, New York
  • 3.45 miLength
  • 11Turns
  • 1956Opened
  • 1961–80F1 US Grand Prix

“The Glen” hosted the Formula 1 United States Grand Prix for twenty straight years. We run the full Boot layout: the uphill Esses where the lap is made, a flat-out back straight into the Bus Stop chicane, then the plunging Boot section before “The 90” brings you home. Rookies, learn the Esses early — commitment there sets up the entire lap.

Week 2 · Grand Prix circuit
Week 2 · Mon Oct 26 · 7:00 PM

Michelin Raceway Road Atlanta

Braselton, Georgia
  • 2.54 miLength
  • 12Turns
  • 1970Opened
  • Since ’98Petit Le Mans

Home of Petit Le Mans. Road Atlanta rewards commitment: the downhill Esses are taken nearly flat in a GT3, and the blind, diving Turn 12 onto the front straight is one of the fastest corners of our season. Nail the 10A/10B chicane and you carry that speed all the way to the line.

Week 3 · Full course
Week 3 · Mon Nov 2 · 7:00 PM

Road America

Elkhart Lake, Wisconsin
  • 4.05 miLength
  • 14Turns
  • 1955Opened
  • ~200 mphFront straight

The longest lap of the season, on one of the few circuits in the world still running its original 1955 layout. Three long straights mean slipstream battles into Turn 1, Turn 5 and Canada Corner, while the Kink is a flat-out act of faith. Braking stability and a clean exit out of the Carousel win here.

Week 4 · Grand Prix circuit
Week 4 · Mon Nov 9 · 7:00 PM

Circuit Zolder

Heusden-Zolder, Belgium
  • 2.49 miLength (4.01 km)
  • 10Turns
  • 1963Opened
  • 10×F1 Belgian GP

Our lone European round. Zolder hosted the Formula 1 Belgian Grand Prix ten times between 1973 and 1984. Two tight chicanes break up the flowing forest sections, so this is a traction-and-exit race: get the power down cleanly out of the chicanes, then defend hard into the Kanaalbocht and Jacky Ickxbocht.

Week 5 · Grand Prix circuit
Week 5 · Mon Nov 16 · 7:00 PM

WeatherTech Raceway Laguna Seca

Monterey, California
  • 2.24 miLength
  • 11Turns
  • 1957Opened
  • 180 ftElevation change

A hundred and eighty feet of elevation change packed into 2.2 miles. The Corkscrew drops roughly five stories through two blind corners — you aim at the sky and trust the car. Short lap, big consequences: track position is everything, so qualifying and a clean Turn 2 matter more here than anywhere else on the calendar.

Week 6 · Full road course · Finale
Week 6 · Mon Nov 23 · 7:00 PM · Season finale

Sonoma Raceway

Sonoma, California
  • 2.52 miLength
  • 12Turns
  • 1969Opened
  • FinaleTrophies awarded

Season finale in wine country. Sonoma’s full 12-turn road course is all elevation and rhythm: the climb to Turn 2, the long, loaded Carousel, and the quick Esses over the crest. There is almost no rest for the tires, so the driver who manages them best over the full race distance lifts the trophy.
