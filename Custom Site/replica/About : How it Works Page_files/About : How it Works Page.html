Our Simulators

Racer

Motion enabled simulator featuring a 57" ultrawide curved display and Simucube direct drive. The perfect rig for sim racing beginners.

Pro

A next level experience of sim racing immersion. Featuring a panoramic triple display, Simucube Pro direct drive, Simtrecs Pro pedals, and GSI Formula wheel.

Ultimate

The ultimate sim racing experience. A no holds bar simulator with 6DOF Qubic motion, triple Samsung ARK 55" displays, Simucube Ultimate direct drive, and Simucube Active pedals. There's nothing else like it.
