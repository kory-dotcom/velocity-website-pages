Frequently Asked

  • Yes, Velocity’s sim racing experience is all about fun for every skill level. Our Pit Crew team is trained to help you select a fun driving experience whether it is your first time or you’re an experienced driver preparing for your next trackday — there’s something for everyone at Velocity.

  • Velocity is a family friendly venue. However, we serve alcohol on premise and ask that parents please keep children under supervision at all times.

  • There is no age requirement to drive at Velocity, however, there is a minimum height recommendation of 5’ or taller to comfortably operate the simulators. If you are close to 5’ we recommend visiting in store before booking online.

  • If you come alone, you can drive solo and challenge the leaderboard. You also have the option to race AI. If you come as a group, you’ll be racing with each other — complete with headsets so you can talk to one another.

  • If you’re planning to eat or drink before your race, we recommend arriving 30–45 minutes before your booking. You can also enjoy food and drinks after your driving session.

  • We have a huge selection of cars spanning different driving series and classes from GT3 to IndyCar and much more. Learn more about available cars on our Cars page.

  • We have virtually every major track in the world including regional tracks from around America and Europe. There are nearly 100+ available tracks. Learn more about tracks at Velocity on our Tracks page.

  • Yes, we have great food & drinks including fresh made flatbreads and craft cocktails & mocktails. Visit our food & drink page to see our full menu.

  • We offer students, service members, and teachers discounts (20% off) available in store only. We also have Eat & Race bundles for 2 drivers (simply purchase additional bundles for larger groups). Lastly, we also offer event packages, to learn more about hosting a large group or event at Velocity, visit our Group Events page.
