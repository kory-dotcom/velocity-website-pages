Frequently Asked Questions

How many guests can a full buyout accommodate?
Our venue accommodates up to 214 guests indoors plus an additional 60 on the outdoor patio, for a total capacity of over 270 guests.
How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 4–6 weeks in advance for full venue buyouts. Popular dates during holidays and weekends fill up quickly, so earlier is always better.
Can I bring my own catering or vendors?
We handle all food and beverage in-house with our craft kitchen and bar. However, we’re happy to work with you on custom menus and can accommodate special dietary requirements. Outside vendors for entertainment or decor can be discussed with your event manager.
What’s the difference between a buyout and a semi-private event?
A full buyout gives you exclusive access to the entire venue — no other guests will be present. Semi-private events reserve a dedicated section of the venue while other guests may be in different areas.
Do you offer AV equipment and presentation capabilities?
Yes. Our venue features comprehensive audio and video systems including large-format displays and sound throughout. Your event manager can help coordinate any additional AV requirements.
