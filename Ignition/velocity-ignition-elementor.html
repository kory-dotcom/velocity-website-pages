01

The threshold

Ignition lives inside Velocity Sim Lounge — a hidden room you reach through the main floor. Ask any team member and the night shifts: quieter, closer, more deliberate.

No signage spectacle. Just a passage into somewhere that feels discovered, not advertised — where the simulators are part of the furniture.

02

The room

Nine full-motion simulators ring the room — close enough to watch a lap unfold, far enough for a real conversation. Backlit arches and warm amber glow replace the urgency of the main floor.

Every detail — the light, the layout, the pace between heats — is designed so the night slows down when you walk in.

03

The pour

Craft cocktails for whatever pace you’re keeping — signatures, classics, and zero-proof options whether you’re on the grid or just watching.
