Ignition is the hidden cocktail lounge at Velocity Sim Racing Lounge in Houston, Texas. Located at 2110 Edwards St in the Heights/Washington Avenue area, Ignition features an elevated full bar, custom fixtures, craft cocktails, and eight motion racing simulators for a unique hidden bar experience. Open Thursday through Sunday evenings. Lounge seating is first come, first served; inquire for private events. 21+ with valid ID.
