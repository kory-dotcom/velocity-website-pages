Ignition is the hidden cocktail lounge at Velocity Sim Racing Lounge in Houston, Texas. Located at 2110 Edwards St in the Heights/Washington Avenue area, Ignition features eight racing simulators alongside craft cocktails, intimate atmosphere, and first-come lounge seating. Open Thursday through Sunday evenings. Book private events through Tripleseat, or book simulator time through Velocity’s booking system. 21+ with valid ID.
