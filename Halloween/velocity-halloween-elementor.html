Good to know

The short version of the fine print.

Is it really free?
Yes. Admission is free and the party is open to the public, all ages. Dallas runs all day. Houston’s $25 tournament is 10am to 1pm, then the party runs 1pm to close. The tournament and the $25 GTR vs Supra meet are Houston only.
How does the costume discount work?
The tournament is Houston only: entry (10am–1pm) drops to $12.50 in costume, and racing is 50% off in costume from 1pm to close. In Dallas, dress up and it’s 50% off all day. A hat alone won’t cut it, and you don’t need to win the contest to get the discount.
Do I need to book?
No. The party, the Dallas all-day discount, and the Houston tournament are all walk-in. The tournament is in person at Velocity Houston only, 10am to 1pm. If you want a guaranteed simulator, book a regular session.
How does the costume contest work?
Two categories — Best Couple Costume and Most Creative Costume — judged at 10pm at both locations. The podium takes home Velocity gift cards.
Is it at Houston and Dallas?
Both host the free party and the 10pm costume contest. The $25 sim racing tournament (10am to 1pm), the GTR vs Supra meet, and the 21+ Spookeasy are Houston only. Dallas is 50% off all day if you dress up.
Is there anything for the 21+ crowd?
In Houston, yes — the Spookeasy in Ignition is a ticketed 21+ party from 7pm to 1am with a welcome cocktail, unlimited racing, light bites, a live DJ and a strolling magician. $45, limited tickets. Dallas guests are welcome, but it’s at the Houston location only.

Dress up. Drive fast.
