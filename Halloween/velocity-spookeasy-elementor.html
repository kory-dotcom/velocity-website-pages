    Good to know

    The short version of the fine print.

    What do I need to get in?
    A ticket and a valid ID. The Spookeasy is strictly 21+ and runs 7pm to 1am in Ignition. Tickets are limited, so grab yours early.
    What’s actually included for $45?
    Your welcome cocktail, unlimited sim racing all night, light bites, the live DJ, the strolling magician (8–10pm), and entry to the costume contest. The specialty Halloween cocktail and mocktail menu is poured all night.
    Do I have to dress up?
    You don’t have to, but you’ll want to. The Spookeasy has its own costume contest — Most Creative and Funniest — judged inside Ignition at 10:30pm, with prizes announced on the night.
    Is this at Houston or Dallas?
    The Spookeasy is Houston only — Ignition at Velocity Houston, 2110 Edwards St in Sawyer Yards. Both locations host the free Fast & Frightening party on the main floor the same night.
    Anything for friends under 21?
    Yes — the main floor at both Velocity Houston and Velocity Dallas hosts the free, all-ages Fast & Frightening party: a $25 walk-in sim racing tournament (50% off in costume), a 10pm costume contest with Velocity gift card prizes, and a candy station. All day in Dallas, 1pm to close in Houston after the $25 GTR vs Supra meet. No ticket needed.
    What do I show at the desk?
    The confirmation page from checkout or the Stripe receipt in your email, under the ticket holder’s name. One order covers everyone on it.
