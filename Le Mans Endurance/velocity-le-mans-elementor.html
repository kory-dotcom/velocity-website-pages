Good to know

The short version before you lock in a stint.

Can I race solo?
Yes. A solo entry is $85. You drive the whole 3 hours and manage your own fuel and tyre stops.
How do I add my teammates?
One person pays for the car and becomes the captain. After checkout you get a Velocity Members account; from your dashboard you invite each driver by email and they claim their seat. Everyone on the car gets event access through November 22.
How do driver swaps work for teams?
Teams swap drivers at pit stops during the race. Use the October 27 practice to rehearse those changes.
Do I need endurance experience?
No. The complimentary practice session is included, and staff will brief you before the race.
What’s included in the practice session?
A 3-hour session on Tuesday, October 27, 2026 at 7:00 PM for setups, driver changes, and race strategy. It is included with every entry.
Which rigs/sims?
You race on the pro sim rigs at the lounge you pick. Your confirmation email has the seating details.
When should I arrive?
Arrive early for the briefing. The exact start time is emailed to entrants.
