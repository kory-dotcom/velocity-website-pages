    How it works

    No booking, no time slot. Monday is first come, first served all day.

    1. Buy online

      Pick how many drivers on each sim, add the main driver’s name, and pay. You’ll get a confirmation page and a Stripe receipt by email.

    2. Walk in Monday

      Come to the lounge you picked any time we’re open on Monday, October 5. Bring your crew — the ticket covers everyone on the order.

    3. Show & race

      Show your ticket at the front desk and we’ll get you on the next open sims. Then go chase lap 1,000,001.

    Good to know

    The short version of the fine print.

    Is it really one day?
    Yes. Tickets are good on Monday, October 5, 2026 only, at Monday’s celebration prices. Tuesday the regular race prices are back.
    Do I need to book a time?
    No. There’s no time slot — walk in whenever suits you on Monday and we’ll seat you on the next open sims.
    Houston or Dallas?
    Both lounges are celebrating. Each runs its own checkout, so pick the lounge you’ll actually visit — a Houston ticket isn’t valid in Dallas and vice versa.
    What do I show at the desk?
    The confirmation page from checkout or the Stripe receipt in your email, under the main driver’s name. One ticket covers everyone on the order.
