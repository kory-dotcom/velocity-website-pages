What members get

More than monthly races

Every plan includes racing you'd otherwise pay for, member pricing on everything else, and a seat at the events we save for members.

Racing sessions every month

4, 6 or 8 Quick Races a month depending on your plan, booked in the app in seconds. Stack two credits for a Double Race, or add named seats and bring friends along.

Member pricing on extra sessions

When your credits are gone, keep racing at 10–20% off every additional session, booked and paid right in the app. No front-desk code, no waiting.

Member pricing at the bar & kitchen

Show the QR code on your digital membership card and pay member prices on food and drinks every visit, whether you're racing that day or just watching.

Live watch parties

Members get in to our live Grand Prix watch parties. Ultimate members get reserved seating up front and a complimentary breakfast for the early-morning races.

Members-only events

Race nights, car rallies, track days and member socials, with priority access and member pricing. They all show up in the app's Events tab with one-tap calendar adds.

Both lounges & early access

Ultimate members race any day at Houston or Dallas on any simulator, and are first in line for new driving experiences, pro coaching and VIP events.
